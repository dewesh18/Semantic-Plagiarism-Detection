Federated Learning is a distributed learning paradigm with two key challenges. The primary challenges in this setup are communication bottleneck and data heterogeneity across edge clients. In this paradigm, edge devices train localized models using proprietary datasets and transmit parameter gradients to a central coordinating aggregation server. The dominant sequence transduction models are based on complex recurrent or convolutional neural networks that include an encoder and a decoder. We propose the Transformer, a novel architecture based solely on self-attention mechanisms to compute representations of its input and output without relying on sequence-aligned RNNs or convolution (Vaswani et al., 2017). Machine learning algorithms discover intricate statistical structures within high-dimensional training distributions by updating weight matrices through backward gradient propagation. This approach provides significant empirical advancements across natural language processing and computer vision applications.
